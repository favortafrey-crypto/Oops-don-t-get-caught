# How does it work
So they game goes like this:
- zombies chase you and you either run from them or punch them to death or else they kill you.
- Every zombie has different number of punches they need inorde to die also, they have different damage potential with the Behemoth beign the most dagerous of all.
- the movement buttons are 
    - "W", "up arrow"; for moving forward
    - "A", "left arrow"; for moving left
    - "S", "down arrow"; for moving backward
    - "D", "right arrow"; for moving right
    - "O", for punching and 
    - "E", to open and close doors.
- A player has to pass through the final door in order to win and no one except me, knows the door for now(I mean as I'm writing this)
- when blood health drops to zero, you die.
